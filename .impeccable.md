## Design Context

### Users

Curious technical readers, peers, and collaborators visit to understand Thibault's work and to find thoughtful writing, projects, and links worth following.

### Brand Personality

Personal, cozy, minimal, clean, and quietly curious. The site should feel like a well-kept notebook rather than a portfolio or publication.

### Aesthetic Direction

A restrained editorial layout with warm neutrals, generous reading space, and small signs of human curation. Both light and dark themes remain supported. Reference qualities include mnot.net's curation, Bas Westerbaan's immediacy, and Sofía Celi's personality, without adopting their density or visual loudness.

### Design Principles

- Put interesting things within one click of the homepage.
- Prefer a few well-chosen trails over exhaustive navigation.
- Use warmth, typography, and language instead of ornamental UI.
- Keep pages fast, accessible, and comfortable on small screens.
- Let the site feel maintained by a person, not generated by a theme.
